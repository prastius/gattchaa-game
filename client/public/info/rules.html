Town rules

Last updated 12 November 2026

Gattchaa Town is a meeting place for collectors. Play fair, be kind, and keep it fun for everyone. Breaking these rules can get you warned, muted, or banned from the town.

1. Be respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, or stalking, in chat, in names, or by following someone around.
  • No hate speech or slurs about anyone's race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexuality, or disability.
  • No sexual, violent or shocking content, and nothing illegal.
  • Don't flood the chat, beg, or advertise other games, shops or services.
  • Don't pretend to be Gattchaa staff, a moderator, or another player.

2. Names, guilds and shops

  • Character names, guild names, guild notices and shop names must follow rule 1. No real people's names used to mock them, and no names that pretend to be staff.
  • Shop names and prices must be honest: don't name a shop after something it doesn't sell to trick people.
  • We may change or remove a name, a guild notice or a shop that breaks the rules.

3. Play fair

  • No cheating. No bots, macros, auto-clickers, modified clients, or tools that play for you or read hidden information.
  • No exploiting bugs. If you find a bug that gives you items, Points, EXP or an unfair edge, don't use it: report it. Using it on purpose can lead to the gains being removed and a ban.
  • Don't attack, overload or interfere with the game's servers or other players' connections.
  • Don't farm with friends to fake wins (in the Battle Pit or the Arena) or titles.

4. Points, trading and real money

  • The town's only money is Gattchaa Points, from your Gattchaa Oripa account. Trade with other players only through the game's own shops in the Market Square.
  • No real-money trading outside Gattchaa. Don't buy, sell or swap characters, accounts, items, Points or services for money or anything else outside the game or Gattchaa's own marketplace.
  • No scams. Don't trick anyone into giving you items or Points, and don't make promises in chat you won't keep. The game can't undo a trade a player agreed to.
  • Real cards are bought and sold only on gattchaaoripa.com, under its own terms.

5. Your account

  • Your character belongs to your Gattchaa Oripa account. Keep your sign-in to yourself: don't share or lend your account.
  • Gattchaa staff will never ask for your password in the game.

6. Fighting other players

  • Players fight each other only where the game allows it: duels, the Battle Pit, and guild wars at Crownhold. That's part of the game; but taunting someone with abuse after a fight still breaks rule 1.

7. What happens if you break the rules

Depending on how serious it is, and whether it happened before, we may: warn you; mute your chat; remove items, Points or titles gained by breaking the rules; close your shop or rename your character or guild; suspend your access to the town for a while; or ban it for good. Serious cases may also affect your Gattchaa Oripa account under its own terms.

8. Reporting

Saw someone break the rules, or found a bug? Report it to Gattchaa Oripa support on gattchaaoripa.com, with the player's name, where and when it happened, and a screenshot if you can.

These rules are part of the Terms. We may update them; the date at the top shows the latest change.
