This policy explains what Gattchaa Town (the "game") keeps about you, why, and what you can do about it. The game is run by [legal name of the company behind Gattchaa Oripa] ("we"). Your Gattchaa Oripa account itself, and everything on gattchaaoripa.com, is covered by the Gattchaa Oripa privacy policy.

Gattchaa Points. Your balance and your payments stay in your Gattchaa Oripa account. The game asks Gattchaa for your balance to show it, and asks Gattchaa to move Points when you buy something. Gattchaa Oripa keeps the record of those payments (with a short description, such as "Red Potion ×10" or "Iron Ingot ×2 from Rina").

Chat. Chat messages are sent to the players who can see them, and the town keeps the last 50 public messages in memory so people who just arrived can catch up. They aren't saved to disk, and they're gone when the server restarts. Party and guild chat isn't kept at all. [If chat is ever logged for moderation, say so here.]

Connection data. Like any website, the server sees your IP address while you're connected. The game uses it only to limit repeated sign-in attempts, and doesn't save it. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs [hosting provider, and how long they keep logs].

We don't ask for your email address, phone number, real name or password, use advertising or tracking cookies, or sell your data.

2. On your device

The game stores a few things in your browser's local storage: your sign-in session, the name and look you last used, and your sound and zoom settings. You can remove them by signing out, or by clearing your browser's data for this site.

3. Who sees it

  • Other players see your character's name, look, level, job, title, guild, shop and what you say in chat, and your place in the rankings.
  • Gattchaa Oripa sees which account is playing and the Points you spend or receive in the game.
  • Our hosting provider stores the game's data on our behalf. [Name the provider and the country where the servers are.]
  • We may share data if the law requires it, or to protect players and the game (for example, against fraud or cheating).

4. How long we keep it

  • Your character and game data: for as long as your account plays the game, and until you ask us to delete it. [Confirm, or set a period after which inactive characters are deleted.]
  • Sign-in sessions: until you sign out, or 30 days at most.
  • Backups of the game's data: [how long backups are kept].

5. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see the game data we keep about you, to correct it, or to delete your character and game data. Contact [privacy contact email] from the email address on your Gattchaa Oripa account. Deleting your game data doesn't delete your Gattchaa Oripa account or your Points; for those, see the Gattchaa Oripa privacy policy.

6. Children

The game is meant for players aged 18 and over (see the Terms). We don't knowingly keep data about younger children. If you think a child is playing, contact us and we'll remove their data.

7. Security

We keep the game's data on our own server, store only hashed sign-in sessions, and talk to Gattchaa Oripa only through its private API with a secret key. No system is perfectly safe; if something goes wrong that affects your data, we'll tell you as the law requires.

8. Changes and contact

We'll update this policy when the game changes what it keeps, and change the date at the top. Questions: [privacy contact email].
