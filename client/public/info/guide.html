How to play

A short guide to Gattchaa Town. Talk to the people in town (press E next to them) for more.

Getting in

  • Gattchaa Town is played with your Gattchaa Oripa account. Press Sign in with Gattchaa on the start screen: you sign in on gattchaaoripa.com, so the game never sees your password.
  • Pick your look and a name, then press Enter town. Your character, level, items and skills are saved to your account, on any device.
  • One window per account: opening a second one takes over from the first.

Money: Gattchaa Points

The town has no money of its own. Everything is paid with the Gattchaa Points in your Gattchaa Oripa account.
  • Points cost: potions and starter gear from Bram, ingots and upgrades from Hilda the Blacksmith, the ferries and ships to the islands, founding a guild, castle guardians, and things in other players' shops.
  • Getting Points: only on gattchaaoripa.com. Monsters drop items and cards, never money, and nobody in town buys anything back.
  • Selling: open your own shop in the Market Square. When something sells, the Points go straight into your Gattchaa account, even while you're away.
  • Your Points show under your health bars (the orange P). Every price is shown before you pay.

Levels and jobs

  • Everyone starts as a Novice. Fight monsters for EXP; each level makes you stronger and gives a skill point (K).
  • Level 10: visit Orin inside the south gate and become a Knight, Mage or Archer.
  • Level 50: pass Orin's Trial of Mastery for your second job (Lord Knight, Wizard or Hunter). Level 80: a second trial for your third job (Paladin, Archmage or Ranger).
  • The highest level is 100. Fainting sends you back to town; jobs lose a little EXP when they faint.

Every land has a boss, and every dungeon has one at the bottom: take a party of four, and step out of the red circles before they hit. The way back from each island is free.

Items, cards and upgrades

  • Monsters drop gear, potions, ingots and loot. Open your bag (I) to wear gear.
  • Every monster has its own rare card. Put cards into gear with free slots for extra bonuses.
  • Hilda the Blacksmith upgrades gear from +0 to +10, for Points and an ingot. Up to +4 always works; after that it can fail and drop one level, but it never breaks. Gear at +7 or more gives you an aura.

Player shops

  • In the Market Square, press Open shop (V), pick up to 12 things from your bag and set a price in Points for each.
  • Your shop stays open for 24 hours after you leave town. To buy, click a shop's sign; Tina, the Market Keeper, can find any item for sale.
  • The real cards you've listed on the Gattchaa marketplace show in your shop too, and are bought on gattchaaoripa.com.

Together

  • Parties (up to 6): click a player and invite them. Nearby members share EXP, with a bonus.
  • Guilds (up to 30): Leopold, east of the fountain plaza, founds one from level 20. Guilds fight for the castle Crownhold every Wednesday and Saturday, 8 to 9 pm Batam time.

Fighting other players

  • Players can only fight each other in a duel (both agree), in the Battle Pit (the red portal by the Arena, from level 20), or in a guild war at Crownhold. Monster lands are always safe from other players.
  • Nobody loses EXP, Points or items to another player. Wins in the Battle Pit and ranked duels in the Arena earn Glory, which Magnus the Arena Master trades for potions, ingots and special gear.
