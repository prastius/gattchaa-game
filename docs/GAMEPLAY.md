# Gattchaa Town: Gameplay Guide

Gattchaa Town is a small online world for card collectors, built around the Gattchaa Oripa shop. You walk around a walled royal town with other players, chat, open your own card shop, and head out of the gates to fight monsters, level up and collect gear, much like Ragnarok Online or TibiaME.

The game has three parts:

1. **Hanging out.** The town is a meeting place. Chat, sit by the fountain, visit the Gattchaa staff, and run your own card shop in the Market Square.
2. **Adventure.** Outside the walls, monsters wait in fields sorted by level. Level up, choose a job, find gear and monster cards, and upgrade your equipment.
3. **Player vs player.** Coming later: duels and an arena.

> **One rule about money.** Real cards are bought and paid for only on gattchaaoripa.com. In-game gold buys in-game things (potions, gear, upgrades) and never real cards.

## 1. Getting started

- **Play as a guest** with just a name, or **Sign in with Gattchaa** using your gattchaaoripa.com account. The sign-in happens on the Gattchaa site, so the game never sees your password.
- **Guests** can do everything except open a shop, but they start over on their next visit.
- **Signed-in players** keep their character, level, bag, gear and skills on any device. Only one window per account is allowed: opening a second one takes over from the first.
- **Your look.** Choose a body, hair, clothes and colours when you join. You can change them any time with the **Wardrobe** button. Gear you wear (helmets, armour, weapons…) shows on top of your look.
- New players appear on the main avenue, between the fountain plaza and the Market Square.

## 3. The town

Gattchaa Town is a walled royal town in the middle of an island, with gates to the west, east and south.

- **Gattchaa Palace** (north) is the Gattchaa store. Oripa machines stand at its doors, one per franchise. Inside are a machine gallery, the Battle Hall, and counters for pickups and the newest packs.
- **The palace forecourt** has the Ichiban Kuji house and the Card Grading house.
- **The fountain plaza** is where the main street crosses the grand avenue. Wandering townsfolk here share tips.
- **The Rooms pavilion** (west of the plaza) has tables showing live Sharing Pack and Packs Battle rooms.
- **The pack stalls** (east of the plaza) are four counters run by Gattchaa staff.
- **The Market Square** (by the south gate) is where players run their own shops.
- **The lakeside park** runs along the west wall, with a pond, benches and a campfire.
- **The Collectors' Café** is in the east quarter.
- **The gates** lead to the monster fields: south to the South Meadow, east to Dusk Hollow.

The town is always safe: monsters never come in, and nobody can fight inside the walls.

**Day and night** follow Batam time. At night the lamps, windows, campfire and shop signs glow and fireflies come out. By day, butterflies fly over the flowers. When someone pulls a big hit on Gattchaa, the whole town hears about it.

## 4. Player shops

Ragnarok-style vending. Signed-in players can sell the cards they have listed on the Gattchaa marketplace.

1. List your cards for sale on gattchaaoripa.com first.
2. Walk into the **Market Square** and press **Open shop** (V).
3. Give your shop a name. The form tells you if your spot is good: not in front of staff, and not so close to another shop that the signs overlap.
4. Your character sits down on a rug with a sign over your head. Anyone can walk up and browse your cards.

- Each card links to its listing on the website, so buying and payment always happen on gattchaaoripa.com.
- When you leave the game, a look-alike minds your shop for **24 hours**.
- Price changes and sold cards update within seconds.
- **Finding shops:** ask **Tina** in the Market Square. She lists every open shop and where it is, and you can browse them straight from her list. Shops also show as orange dots on the minimap.

### How fighting works

- **Click a monster** (or press Space) and your character walks up and attacks it about once a second, as long as it's in reach. Bows and staves attack from a distance.
- **Missing.** You land 95% of your hits against monsters at or below your level. Each level the monster is above you takes 4% off, down to 60%.
- **Critical hits.** 5% of hits are critical: they ignore defence and do 1.5× damage. Some cards and skills raise this chance.
- **Defence** softens hits but never blocks them completely. Defence equal to the attack halves a hit.
- **Skills** always hit and never miss.

### Rewards

- **EXP** is shared by everyone who hurt the monster, by how much damage each person did. So a party sharing a boss all get EXP.
- **Gold** goes to whoever did the most damage.
- **Easy monsters give less.** A monster more than 5 levels below you gives 15% less EXP for each extra level, down to 10%.
- **Level-ups** heal you fully. Every 10th level is announced in chat.
- The level cap is **50** for now and will be raised step by step.

### Healing and fainting

- Out of combat you slowly get HP and MP back: a full heal takes about a minute.
- **Sit down** (X) to heal 3× faster. Resting **inside the town walls** doubles that again.
- **Potions** heal straight away, even in the middle of a fight: one every 0.8 seconds. Q drinks the smallest potion that heals you fully.
- **Fainting.** At 0 HP you faint and wake up back in town, fully healed. Novices lose nothing. Knights, Mages and Archers lose 3% of a level's EXP.

### Field boss: the Pumpking

The Pumpking lives where the east road ends in Dusk Hollow. It's a Ragnarok-style MVP:

- There's only one. It has 12,000 HP, breathes fire from a distance, and heals slowly if left alone.
- It's drawn big, with its name and health bar always showing.
- When it falls, the whole town hears who brought it down. It comes back **10 minutes** later, and the town hears that too.
- A level 20 Knight can just about beat it alone with plenty of potions. A party of three takes about 80 seconds.
- **It always drops** King's Candy (sells for 600 gold), a Mithril Ingot and usually an Orange Potion. It often drops good gear (Crusader Shield, Greathelm, Great Bow, Ember Staff, Knight's Blade), and half the time a Star Crystal. Rarely it drops the **Moonblade** (8%) or the legendary **Pumpking Card** (2%).

Everyone starts as a **Novice**. At **level 10**, visit **Orin the Job Master** just inside the south gate and choose a job. **A job is for life**: you can't change it later.

These numbers are without any gear. Your stats grow automatically every level; there are no stat points to spend.

### Skill points

- You get **1 skill point** at the job change, and **1 more every level** after that.
- Spend them in the skills window (**K**). Each skill goes up to **level 10** and gets stronger every level.
- Use skills with keys **1–6** or the skill buttons. Each skill costs MP and has a cooldown, shown as a shadow on its button. There's also a short pause (0.7 s) between any two skills.
- Aimed skills use your target. Without one, they pick the nearest monster and walk you into range.
- Attack skills don't work inside the town.

## 7. Items and equipment

### Your bag and gear

- **Eight equipment slots:** weapon, shield, head, armour, legs, feet, ring and amulet. The first six show on your character for everyone to see.
- **The bag** holds 30 stacks. Potions, loot, ingots and cards stack up to 99; equipment doesn't stack.
- Click an item in the bag to see it. Double-click to wear it or drink it. You can drop things on the ground for anyone to pick up.
- Some gear needs a minimum level, and some is only for certain jobs.

## 8. Monster cards

Ragnarok style: every monster very rarely drops **its own card**. A card goes into a free card slot on a piece of equipment and stays there **for good**. Cards never come back out.

| Slime Card | Slime | Armour | Bouncy | HP +30 | 1 in 1,250 |
| Grass Snake Card | Grass Snake | Feet | Slithering | Evade +8% (monsters miss you more) | 1 in 1,250 |
| Hornet Card | Hornet | Weapon | Stinging | ATK +2, Critical +8% | 1 in 1,000 |

- **Card slots.** Most gear has one slot and the better weapons have two. In the bag, small dots in the corner of an item show its slots: hollow when free, gold when filled. The Leather Cap, Leather Pants, Copper Ring and Clover Charm have no slots.

- **News.** A card fresh from a monster is announced to the whole town.
- **Evade** can't go above 50%, however many cards you stack.

## 9. The Blacksmith: upgrades and auras

**Hilda the Blacksmith** works by her forge fire outside the south gate. She makes your gear stronger, from **+0 to +10**.

### How upgrading works

- Every try costs **gold** and **one ingot**.
- **+1 to +4 always work.** After that, a try can fail.
- **A failed try drops the gear one level** (+6 fails and becomes +4). **It never breaks**, and you never lose the item.
- Upgrading works on weapons, shields, helmets, armour, legs and boots. **Rings and amulets can't be upgraded.**

| Upgrade to | Chance | Fee (gold) | Ingot used |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +1 | 100% | 100 | Iron Ingot |
| +2 | 100% | 200 | Iron Ingot |
| +3 | 100% | 300 | Iron Ingot |
| +4 | 100% | 400 | Iron Ingot |
| +5 | 90% | 600 | Iron Ingot |
| +6 | 75% | 900 | Mithril Ingot |
| +7 | 60% | 1,200 | Mithril Ingot |
| +8 | 45% | 1,600 | Mithril Ingot |
| +9 | 35% | 2,000 | Star Crystal |
| +10 | 25% | 2,500 | Star Crystal |

### What each level adds

- **Weapons:** more ATK every level, about an eighth of the weapon's own ATK (at least +1). For example, a Saber gets ATK +2 per level, a Knight's Blade +3 and a Moonblade +4. A +10 Moonblade has ATK +72 instead of +32.
- **Everything else:** DEF +1 per level.

### Ingots

| Ingot | Used for | Where to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iron Ingot | +1 to +5 | Hilda sells them (100 gold). Hornets, Snapper Flowers, Grubs, Night Bats and Rock Worms drop them |
| Mithril Ingot | +6 to +8 | Dusk Hollow monsters (Rock Worms most often), rarely Snapper Flowers. The Pumpking always drops one |
| Star Crystal | +9 and +10 | Gazers and Ghosts (about 1 in 200 to 250), and the Pumpking (one kill in two) |

### What it really costs

Because failures drop the gear back down, high upgrades take many tries. On average:

| Goal | Tries | Gold | Mithril Ingots | Star Crystals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +7 | about 10 | about 6,000 | about 4 | 0 |
| +9 | about 29 | about 32,000 | about 19 | about 3 |
| +10 | about 74 | about 100,000 | about 50 | about 16 |

### Auras

Wear any piece of gear at **+7 or higher** and everyone sees an aura around you:

| Best upgrade you wear | Aura |
| --- | --- |
| +7 or +8 | A blue ring at your feet, with a few sparks |
| +9 | A violet double ring, with sparks rising |
| +10 | A golden ring with ripples and a column of light |

At night, auras glow like the street lamps. A successful **+9 or +10** is announced to the whole town.

Upgraded gear shows its level in its name ("+7 Double Stinging Saber") and in the corner of its bag slot. It keeps its level if you drop it and pick it up again.

## 11. A good path for new players

1. **Level 1–4:** Slimes by the road, south of town. Buy a few Red Potions and a Dagger or Short Sword from Bram.
2. **Level 4–7:** Grass Snakes in the west grass. Wear what you find.
3. **Level 7–10:** Hornets in the east field. They attack on sight, so keep potions ready (Q).
4. **Level 10:** Visit **Orin** just inside the south gate and choose your job. Spend your first skill point (K).
5. **Level 10–14:** Snapper Flowers by the lake, or Grubs and Night Bats out of the **east gate**.
6. **Level 14–20:** Rock Worms, Gazers and Ghosts deeper in Dusk Hollow. Use skills on Ghosts, since plain attacks often miss them.
7. **Level 20+:** Team up for the **Pumpking**. Save your Mithril and Star Crystals for **Hilda**, and aim for your first aura at +7.

Sell your monster loot to Bram whenever your bag fills up, and sit down (X) to heal faster between fights.
